# Product Guidelines: Be-Productive (Fluxo)

## Prose & Communication
- **Empathetic & Direct:** Communication should be professional and encouraging, while remaining clear and authoritative to guide users effectively.
- **Inspirational:** The platform's voice should inspire users to achieve their goals and maintain a healthy digital life.

## UI/UX Principles
- **Minimalist Design:** Focus on reducing cognitive load and visual noise to support focus and mental clarity.
- **Anti-Addiction Patterns:** Proactively design features that prevent addictive scrolling and promote intentional usage.
- **Context-Aware Feedback:** Provide clear visual and functional signals when switching between productivity and leisure modes.

## Documentation Standards
- **ADR Driven:** All major architectural decisions must be documented through Architecture Decision Records.
- **API Documentation:** Comprehensive JSDoc (for TypeScript) and GoDoc (for Go) are required for all public-facing APIs.
- **Visual Architecture:** Use dark-mode Mermaid diagrams to document system structure and data flows.

## Engineering Standards
- **Clean Architecture & SOLID:** Strict adherence to Clean Architecture principles and SOLID design patterns.
- **Mandatory TDD:** A Red-Green-Refactor workflow is mandatory for all production code changes.
- **High Quality Assurance:** Maintain a minimum of 80% test coverage and ensure zero linting warnings across all services.
