# Be-Productive (Fluxo)

## Vision
Be-Productive is a social network focused on mental health that balances productivity and well-being. It aims to reduce social media addiction and promote intentional usage patterns through ethical recommendation algorithms and integrated focus tools.

## Target Audience
- **Knowledge Workers:** Professionals who need to stay productive without sacrificing their mental health.
- **Academic Students:** Students balancing intense study periods with necessary, healthy leisure.
- **Well-being Seekers:** Individuals looking for a more mindful and less addictive social media experience.

## Core Goals
- **Reduce Addiction:** Combat the "infinite scroll" and other engagement-maximizing dark patterns.
- **Promote Intentional Use:** Encourage users to set and follow daily productivity and leisure goals.
- **Ethical AI Feed:** Leverage AI to recommend content that supports the user's current emotional state and goals.

## Key Features
- **Well-being Feed:** An adaptive content stream that adjusts its ranking based on user stress and emotional signals.
- **Focus Sessions:** Dedicated periods for deep work with minimized distractions and "absolute mode" capabilities.
- **Smart Communities:** AI-supported groups that foster mutual support and collaborative productivity.

## Success Metrics
- **Goal Completion Rate:** Tracking the percentage of users achieving their daily focus and well-being targets.
- **Well-being Score:** Measuring the qualitative improvement in user-reported mental health and stress levels.
- **Time Well Spent:** Maximizing the ratio of intentional vs. passive time spent on the platform.
